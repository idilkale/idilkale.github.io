Ongoing research · Arizona State University

What should AI do after you say no?

When you turn down an AI's suggestion, the next move matters.

Does it push again, back off, or explain itself? I'm studying how different post-refusal strategies shape people's sense of autonomy, their trust, and their willingness to keep working with the system.

  • Conversational AI
  • Autonomy
  • Trust
  • Psychological reactance
  • Interaction design

Research & development · Arizona State University

What if learners owned their AI's memory?

Archie is an AI-native learning companion where the learner, not the platform, owns their context.

I design the interactions and build the harness behind them: a RAG pipeline over course material, an agent, and the guardrails that keep the learner in the driver's seat.

  • AI in education
  • Agentic systems
  • RAG
  • AI harnesses
  • Learner-owned memory

Can learning in VR feel like learning together?
