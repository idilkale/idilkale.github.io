About

Movement you can almost feel

A 90-second descent into deep water, built in After Effects. Thin white lines drift, tangle and swell like currents, seahorses and coral surface out of the dark, and short lines of text float past as you sink.

“Slowly descend in the ocean so deep … floating in space, feeling oh so free.”

I wanted the motion, not any single frame, to carry the mood: slow, weightless and a little uneasy.
