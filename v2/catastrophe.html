The series

20 images, one feeling

Catastrophe imagines everyday life as the climate breaks down: dust storms, locust swarms, floods, wildfires, oceans full of plastic and land too dry to farm. I generated the images in Midjourney, refined them in Photoshop, and narrowed 30 down to these 20, keeping the ones where ordinary people are caught in the middle of it.

It's a companion to Crisis Type: the same subject, told through images instead of letters.
