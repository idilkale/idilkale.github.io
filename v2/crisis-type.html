01

The idea

Type that reacts to what it says

Crisis Type is a coded typography project about climate change and global warming. Words aren't just written, they're transformed by their meaning.

I sketched the first forms in Illustrator, then coded a font-like system that reacts to words such as crisis, global warming, planet and carbon dioxide.

02

Letters under pressure

Unstable on purpose

Letters stretch, overlap, rotate, scatter and break apart to show the tension of an environmental crisis.

Turning language into unstable forms lets the type itself carry the urgency, pressure and chaos of climate change.
