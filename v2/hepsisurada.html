Overview

A marketplace, from the database up

Hepsisurada, a playful nod to Hepsiburada, is an online clothing store with three kinds of users. Shoppers browse and sort products, save a wishlist, fill a cart, pay and track their orders, while product managers and sales managers each get their own dashboard behind the scenes.

Login runs on JSON Web Tokens, and the session and cart live in shared React context, so they follow you from page to page.
