E-waste sculpture · 2025

Friend

A small alien we built entirely out of things that had already stopped working.

Timeline
February 2025
My role
Designer & Maker
Tools
Soldering iron, hot glue, salvaged electronics
Team
Duo project, made with a friend
01

The parts

A box of things that no longer worked

We started with a box of dead parts and kept asking what each piece could still become.

  • A milk frotherbecame the torso
  • Old resistors, taped end to endbecame arms
  • A car key fobbecame a head, its soldered wires standing up like hair, or maybe antennae; we never quite agreed which
  • A ring of old keyboard keysspells out its name
02

The name

It started looking back at us

We weren't trying to make it do anything. We just wanted to see what happens when you treat spare parts like they deserve a second life instead of a drawer.

By the time we'd glued its name on, it had stopped feeling like junk and started feeling like it was looking back at us. So we called it Friend.
