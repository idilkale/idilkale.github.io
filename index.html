What I do

I design digital products, and lately I'm most curious about the moments where people and AI meet. Some days that means sitting in on a usability test and watching where someone gets stuck. Other days it means figuring out how a system can earn someone's trust instead of just asking for it.

How I do it

Research shows me where people get stuck. Design is my best guess at getting them unstuck. Testing tells me if I was right, and then I go around again. Having studied both computer science and visual design, I feel at home on both sides of that loop.

Why it matters

AI is moving into the tools we use every day. It's not about making it smarter; it's about making sure people still feel in charge. For me, that means being honest about what the system is doing, and respectful when someone wants to do things their own way.

Who I am

People describe me as..

Curious

I’d rather ask one more question than ship one more screen. Most of my best design decisions started with someone saying, “wait, why does it work like that?”

Playful

I always have something on the side that has nothing to do with work. Play is how I find ideas I would never find on purpose.

Detail-obsessed

A label that finally makes sense, a button in the right place: small details are what make something feel right. I’ll happily spend an afternoon on one.

In-between

I code like a designer and design like an engineer. Most of my work happens where those two meet.

Honest

I’d rather hear “this doesn’t work” in a usability test than after launch, and I try to give feedback the same way: clearly, and kindly.
