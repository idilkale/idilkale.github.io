What I do

I design digital products end to end, from research to interface to brand, with a growing focus on how people and AI work together, and how to build trust between them.

How I do it

Research shows me where people get stuck, design is my best guess at getting them unstuck, and testing tells me if I was right. With a double major in Computer Science & Engineering and Visual Communication Design, I'm comfortable on both sides of that loop.

Why it matters

AI is moving into the tools we use every day. Good design now means making them honest too: clear about what the system is doing, and respectful when people want to do things their own way.
