About

A mark of my own

Letters fused into single marks: one letter's stroke turns into part of the next, and negative space keeps both readable. I drew them in Illustrator as a framed set of three, in a high-contrast serif with sharp, calligraphic turns.

The challenge was balance: two letters sharing one shape without either one disappearing.
